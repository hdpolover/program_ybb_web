Fix Website Favicons

Click the button below to generate proper favicon files for your website. This will fix the issue with the default CodeIgniter favicon showing in Google search results.

Generate Favicons

If you prefer to upload a custom logo for your favicon, use the custom favicon generator instead.

Instructions:

  1. Click the "Generate Favicons" button above to create favicon files from your existing logo.
  2. Check that favicon files are created in your website's root and assets/images directories.
  3. The favicon markup has already been added to your website's header template.
  4. Wait for Google to re-crawl your website (may take a few days).
